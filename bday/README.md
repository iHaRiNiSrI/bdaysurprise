# 🎂 Umang's Birthday Website | Step-by-Step Experience

A romantic, screen-by-screen (page-after-page) Birthday Web App created with **React**, **Tailwind CSS**, **Lucide Icons**, and **Canvas Confetti**.

---

## 🚀 How to Open and Test:

1. Open your File Explorer and navigate to: `D:\bday`
2. **Double-click `index.html`** to open it immediately in Chrome, Edge, or Brave.
3. Click the **"Test / Preview"** button at the top right to unlock and test all screens!

---

## 🧭 Page-by-Page Journey:

- **Screen 0: The Vault Lock & Countdown**
  * Live countdown to 11:55 PM for Umang.
  * Glowing unlock button that transitions to Chapter 1, starts playing Beethoven's *Für Elise*, and triggers confetti.

- **Screen 1: Chapter 1 — How We First Met**
  * Focused card with photo, date (*The Beginning of Us*), and the story of meeting Umang.
  * Navigation arrow: *Next Chapter ➔*

- **Screen 2: Chapter 2 — The Day I Proposed to You (12th Dec 2024)** 💍
  * Focused card with photo, date (*December 12, 2024*), and the heartfelt story of you proposing to him.
  * Navigation arrows: *⬅️ Previous* and *Next Chapter ➔*

- **Screen 3: Chapter 3 — Where We Stand Today**
  * Focused card with photo, date (*Here, With You, Always*), and your love note.
  * Navigation arrows: *⬅️ Previous* and *Next: Vault of Wishes ➔*

- **Screen 4: The Mystery Key & 4 Chests Game**
  * 4 enchanted chests for Umang.
  * Clicking a chest pops up his **Destiny Wish** highlighted in gold, plus a summary of all 4 wishes.
  * Navigation arrow: *Continue to Your Birthday Cake 🎂 ➔*

- **Screen 5: The Velvet Rose & Cream Cake (Grand Climax)**
  * Handcrafted 3-tier Velvet Rose cake with flickering candles, inscribed with *"Umang"* and *"Forever My Baby"*.
  * Interactive button: *"Blow Out The Candles! 🎂"*.
  * Extinguishes flames, triggers multi-stage **fullscreen confetti and party poppers**, and reveals your grand love letter!
  * Replay button to restart the journey anytime.

---

## ⌨️ Navigation Controls:
- **Floating Bottom Step Bar**: Shows steps `[1] [2] [3] [4] [5]` with **Back** and **Next** buttons.
- **Side Arrow Buttons**: Floating Chevron arrows on the left and right of the screen.
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Press **Left Arrow (←)** or **Right Arrow (→)** on your keyboard to navigate between pages!

---

## 📸 Adding Your Own Couple Photos:
1. Open the folder: `D:\bday\photos`
2. Paste 3 photos named:
   * `chapter1.jpg`
   * `chapter2.jpg`
   * `chapter3.jpg`
3. Refresh `index.html` in your browser!
