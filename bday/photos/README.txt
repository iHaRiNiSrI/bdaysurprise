HOW TO ADD YOUR COUPLE PHOTOS HERE:
====================================

You can easily replace the pictures in the 3 chapters with your own photos!

1. Pick 3 of your favorite photos with Umang:
   - Photo 1: When you first met / early days
   - Photo 2: The proposal day (12th Dec 2024) or a favorite trip/date
   - Photo 3: A recent sweet photo of both of you

2. Copy your 3 pictures into this exact folder (D:\bday\photos) and name them:
   - chapter1.jpg (or chapter1.png)
   - chapter2.jpg (or chapter2.png)
   - chapter3.jpg (or chapter3.png)

3. Refresh index.html in your browser, and your own photos will appear automatically!
   (If you haven't added them yet, the website automatically displays beautiful romantic placeholders so it always looks stunning).
